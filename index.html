Keep your proof together, before you need it.

Claimfolio keeps a dated record of your business’s equipment and stock — a photo, the date, and the receipt when you have it — so that after a loss you can show your insurer what you had, from evidence instead of memory.

Coming to the App Store (iPhone and iPad, iOS 18 or later) and Google Play (Android 8 or later). Not yet released.

What it does

  • Capture an item with a photo, its name, a category, where it is kept and the date. Attach the receipt now, or skip it and add it later.
  • The ledger lists every item, newest first, and filters by location. Items without a receipt are marked.
  • The monthly digest shows which of the month’s items are still missing a receipt, oldest gap first.
  • Export the whole ledger as a PDF (one page and photo per item) or a CSV, in one tap, and choose where it goes.

Free, then one $4.99 unlock, and on your phone only

Claimfolio is free to download, and the free version does everything for up to 10 entries. One $4.99 in-app purchase removes the limit for good: pay once, no subscription, no advertising, and nothing you have saved is ever locked. There is no account and no sign-in. The app itself sends nothing anywhere (only buying or restoring the unlock talks to the App Store or Google Play); your ledger stays on your phone and is included in your phone’s own backup if you have backup turned on (see the privacy policy).

What it does not do

Claimfolio never estimates what a claim is worth and never contacts your insurer. You review and submit every claim yourself. It is a record-keeping tool, not insurance or advice.
