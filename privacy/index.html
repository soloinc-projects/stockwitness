Privacy policy

StockWitness, by 8086 LLC · Effective 18 October 2026

In short: StockWitness keeps your inventory on your phone. It has no account, no server and no network access, so it cannot send us — or anyone — your ledger. 8086 LLC receives nothing from the app.

What the app keeps, and where

DataWhere it lives
Item names, categories, dates, locations you type, notes, and any value you enter yourselfIn the app’s private storage on your phone
Photos of items and receipt images you take or chooseIn the app’s private storage on your phone. Photos you choose from your library are copied in; the app does not read the rest of your library
A short technical report if the app crashesIn the app’s private storage on your phone. It is never sent
Your age range, where the store provides itRead from Apple’s or Google’s age signal at launch and held in memory only; never stored, exported or sent

The app uses your operating system’s standard file protection. It adds no encryption of its own.

Backups

If iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Android backup to your Google account is turned on, your phone includes your StockWitness ledger in that backup and restores it to a new phone. That copy is held by Apple or Google under your account; StockWitness and 8086 LLC never see it. Turn backup off in your phone’s settings if you would rather the ledger stayed only on the phone.

When a file leaves your phone

Only when you export the ledger (PDF or CSV) and choose where it goes — for example Files, an email or a message. Where it goes after that is up to you.

What the app never collects

No account details, no analytics or usage data, no advertising identifiers, no location from GPS, no contacts, no payment data, no biometrics. The app contains no third-party SDK that collects data. Permissions: on iPhone, the camera, used only when you take a photo; on Android, none — photos come through the system photo picker and camera.

Deleting your data

Delete an item in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it keeps on the phone. To remove the copy in your phone’s backup, delete it from your iCloud or Google account backups. There is nothing held by us to delete. See Deleting your data.

Children

StockWitness is a business record-keeping tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: support@8086.llc, 8086 LLC, 372 5th Ave, New York, NY 10018, United States.
