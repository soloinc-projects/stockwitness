Deleting your data

Claimfolio has no account, no sign-in and no server, so there is no account to delete and nothing held by 8086 LLC.

On your phone

  • To delete one item: open it in the ledger and choose Delete.
  • To delete everything: delete the Claimfolio app. Its private storage — every entry, photo, receipt image, note and crash report — is removed with it.

In your phone’s backup

If iCloud Backup or Android backup was on, a copy of the ledger may be in that backup. Delete it from your iCloud or Google account’s backups.
